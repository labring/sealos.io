For a group of friends, choose hosting around three practical questions: when
everyone can join, what the complete service costs, and who will look after the
world. A hosted server suits a group that plays at different times. Shared World
suits a session organized around one available host. A home computer or DIY VPS
suits someone willing to maintain the runtime, network access and recovery
copies.

A low advertised monthly price is useful for building a shortlist. The purchase
decision also needs the server's actual allocation, your group's activities and
the work involved in getting back online after a problem. Mining near an
existing base and exploring new terrain put different demands on a server.

This guide is published by Sealos, one of the providers being compared. Its
selection criteria are the total hosting payment, the declared multiplayer
experience, setup and recurring work, and demonstrated recovery. **Documentary
Evidence** means a dated provider statement. **Practice Evidence** means an
observed operation with its configuration and measurement scope recorded.

The [first-join tutorial](/blog/eaglercraft-server/) already verifies the
client-versus-server version distinction and the distinction between an
application restart and a recovery from a separate copy. This guide keeps both
distinctions and applies them to the buying decision.

## What a Complete Hosting Offer Needs

An Eaglercraft browser session connects to a game server through a WebSocket
gateway. A browser client delivered over HTTPS needs a compatible secure WSS
connection. Hosting the client page, running Paper, exposing the gateway and
storing the world are distinct parts of that arrangement.

Check the game version at both ends. A browser client labeled 1.8 and a server
package labeled 2.2.7 refer to different version schemes. The Sealos package's
demonstrated path uses the 1.8 client with Paper 1.8.8. A provider's ability to
change its Paper version needs verification with the gateway and authentication
configuration that your friends will actually use.

The Sealos template packages the client, gateway, Paper and web administration
interface together. Players create their own accounts; the administrator keeps
the management password private. With any alternative, confirm which components
are supplied, which you install, and how a friend gets from the shared link to
authenticated play. Include required domains, ports and other extras in the
cost.

Eagler.Host advertises free hosting with resource upgrades earned through

reserves resource changes and termination, and assigns backup responsibility to
the world owner. Check the actual allocation, version and retention conditions
in your account. These are provider statements checked September 14, 2026.

Shared World is a choice for a live gathering with a host present. The
[first-join tutorial](/blog/eaglercraft-server/) describes that branch; verify
the sharing support of the particular client you use. Its session model matters
when friends expect to return at different times throughout the week.

For a home machine, count electricity using your measured average power draw:
kilowatts multiplied by operating hours and your electricity rate. For example,
an assumed 50 W for 720 hours uses 36 kWh. At an assumed $0.20/kWh, that is
$7.20 for electricity. Hardware, connectivity and maintenance add their own
costs. Those inputs are an illustration to replace with your own measurements.

is a documentary DIY option. Check account eligibility, available capacity,
instance architecture and idle-resource rules before planning around it. A VM
still needs a compatible server, gateway, network configuration and
maintenance.

## Understand the Payment Before Comparing Resources

Compare the cash a new buyer must spend to keep the complete service running
for the stated billing period. Record currency, region, quantity, mandatory
extras, taxes and payment fees. Monthly equivalents for annual purchases should
sit beside the actual amount due at checkout.

Use three separate price labels:

- **Introductory Price:** the offer available under specified eligibility and
  duration conditions.
- **Regular Listed Price:** the provider's public ordinary price on the source
  date. Confirm the particular subscription's renewal terms separately.
- **Actual Hosting Charge:** the amount charged for the tested purchase,
  including its billing period, extras and applicable fees or taxes.

## How Sealos Plan and Allocation Costs Fit Together

A **Resource Plan** supplies the account's resource pool. **Server Allocation**
assigns part of that pool to this instance. Account capacity already committed
to other applications affects what remains available for your world.

On September 14, 2026, [Sealos pricing](https://sealos.io/pricing/) lists
Starter at **$7/month for eligible first paid-plan purchases**, with **$34/month
as the Regular Listed Price**. Hobby lists **$25/month introductory** and

Starter's listed pool is 2 vCPU, 2 Gi RAM, 10 Gi disk and 10 GB traffic. The

For a new buyer, the full required plan payment belongs in the comparison. For
an existing customer, calculate an incremental case from the resources already
available. A server that fits within an already-paid pool can add zero plan
payment for that billing period; check usage charges, limits and any required
upgrade for the actual account. Keep that case labeled as existing-account use.

Traffic, disk and memory need their own headroom. World exploration creates
additional terrain files, and player activity transfers data through the public
connection. Record observed growth and usage with their activity window before
projecting a month. Storage reserved for an independent recovery copy needs its

## Results for Two, Five and Ten Players

A **Concurrent Player Workload** specifies how many authenticated players are
active together and what they do. A **Comparable Game Workload** keeps the game
version, starting world and activities consistent across providers.

The declared steady-activity threshold is TPS of at least 19 at a minimum of
95% of sampled points. Each evaluated workload requires five minutes of warm-up
and at least thirty minutes of observation, repeated at least twice. Existing
terrain activity and new-chunk generation are reported separately.

The **Lowest Passing Offer** for a player target is the lowest tested or
non-excluded purchase option that meets the declared experience and functional
requirements. Keep the candidate trail with the result: a configured limit of
20 player slots describes a setting, while a ten-player result requires those
players to participate for the measured workload.

## Setup, Availability and World Recovery

Measure setup from the same starting point on both providers. Active operator
time includes choosing options, configuring access and completing the first
join. Elapsed time also includes provisioning and startup waits. Record each
separately so readers can compare hands-on effort and time until they can play.

Describe ordinary management work concretely: inviting a player, saving the
world, adjusting resources, renewing service and exporting a recovery copy.
For availability, pair provider idle terms with the actual observation window
and rejoin checks. Hosting Haven's sales page and
[SLA](https://hostinghaven.us/service-level-agreement) use different
availability figures and scopes; establish which conditions apply to the
purchased service.

A **Persistent World** survives ordinary restarts while its storage is
retained. A **Recovery Copy** is held separately from that working storage. A
**Restored World** is recovered onto replacement storage and checked through
real play.

The [first-join tutorial](/blog/eaglercraft-server/) verified registration,
returning login and a gold block retained across a restart on the original
volume. The comparison's recovery check extends that evidence: export a
separate copy, restore it independently, then verify a known marker, the
required player's identity and inventory, the existing login and browser
re-entry.

Keep copies that include world files, player state and the authentication data
needed for continuity. Protect exported credentials and account databases. A
restore rehearsal tells you which files, commands and access you would need
after losing the working storage. The complete step-by-step migration procedure
will live in a separate recovery tutorial when it is published.

## Choose Around Your Group's Next Month of Play

Start with the largest simultaneous group you expect, its activities and your
acceptable administration effort. Use that workload's passing candidate and
actual purchase cost. A friend who explores new terrain continuously can change
the demands on the same world; revisit the allocation when play patterns
change.

For occasional sessions around one host, evaluate Shared World. For a strict
zero-rental budget, review free-hosting conditions and the work a DIY option
requires. For a hosted world with less setup work, compare the measured paid
